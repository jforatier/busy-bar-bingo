# 13 — Legal

*This is the project's own analysis, not legal advice.*

## No money, no gambling feature

Bingo and loto are games of chance; played for money they are regulated almost everywhere (licences, charity-only loto nights in France, age limits). The app only **calls numbers and keeps the board**: no stakes, no payment, no prizes, no accounts, no network. How a game night is run, and whether it is lawful where it takes place, is the organiser's business. The catalog texts never present the app as a way to win money.

## The voice

The clips are synthetic speech; no person's voice was recorded or imitated.

| Part | Licence | What it asks of us |
|---|---|---|
| Kokoro-82M weights and voice files (hexgrad) | Apache License 2.0 | nothing for the audio it produces; the model itself is not redistributed (it stays outside the repo, `voice_gen.py` says where to get it). Credited in the README |
| Training data of `af_heart` | "permissive/non-copyrighted audio" (Kokoro model card) | nothing |
| Training data of `ff_siwis`: the SIWIS French Speech Synthesis Database (Yamagishi, Honnet, Garner, Lazaridis, University of Edinburgh, 2017, doi:10.7488/ds/1705) | CC BY 4.0 | the dataset is not redistributed, only speech from a model trained on it; we credit it anyway, with its authors, licence and link, in the README and here |
| Generation tools: `kokoro-onnx` (MIT), ONNX Runtime (MIT), eSpeak NG through `espeakng-loader` (GPL-3.0, turns text into phonemes) | as stated | used on the maintainer's computer only, never shipped; their licences cover the programs, not the audio they help produce |

To change a voice, run `scripts/voice_gen.py` again with a model whose licence, and whose voice's training data, allow redistribution of the output; update this table in the same change.

## Trademarks

"Bingo" and "loto" are generic game names. The French state lottery's brands are not used. "BUSY" appears only to describe the device the app runs on; the app is named Bingo.

## Privacy

The app stores the game in progress on the bar (`localStorage`) and sends nothing anywhere.
