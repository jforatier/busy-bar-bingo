## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-27

### Highlights

- Run a bingo night from the bar: US 75-ball or 90-ball (UK and French loto), never the same number twice.
- The drum spins, the ball rolls out and a caller's voice announces it, in English or French.
- The host's board on the back display: every called number, to check a winning card at a glance.
- Manual draws on the Start button, or automatic draws at a steady pace.
- The game is saved after every ball: quitting the app never loses it.

### Added

- The draw: a no-repeat draw for the 75-ball and 90-ball games, the prizes in order, and a saved game that survives quitting.
- Screens: an animated title (the five balls spelling BINGO), the new-game choice (75 or 90 balls), the spinning cage the ball rolls out of, the called ball, the history on the wheel, the win check and its celebration, the game over.
- The host's board on the back display, in the app's own pixel digits.
- Manual and automatic draws (settings `mode`, `auto_pace`), and synthesized sounds (setting `sounds`).
- A caller's voice in English or French for every ball and prize (settings `voice`, `language`), and French prize names on screen.
- Traditional nicknames of the numbers, scrolling under the number after the call (setting `nicknames`, off by default).
- Launcher icons: a blue bingo ball.
